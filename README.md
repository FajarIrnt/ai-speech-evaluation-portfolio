# AI-Generated Speech Evaluation

## Project Overview

This personal portfolio project compares two pairs of self-generated text-to-speech (TTS) audio samples using the same English text for each pair.

The objective is to assess audio quality, naturalness, pronunciation, and contextual appropriateness through structured listening and comparative evaluation.

All audio samples were created specifically for this project. No confidential company materials or internal task recordings were used.

## Example 1: Professional Meeting

### Test Text

> Good morning! Thank you for joining today's meeting. I'll explain the three main points, then we can discuss the next steps together.

### Evaluation Method

Both audio samples were evaluated using the same criteria:

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| Audio Quality | Clarity and overall sound quality |
| Naturalness | Natural-sounding speech and intonation |
| Pronunciation | Clarity and correctness of spoken words |
| Contextual Appropriateness | Suitability for a professional meeting |

Each sample was listened to and compared directly. No human reference audio was used.

### Audio Samples

[Listen to Voice A](samples/voice_a.mp3)

[Listen to Voice B](samples/voice_b.mp3)

### Evaluation Results

| Criterion | Audio A | Audio B |
|---|---|---|
| Audio Quality | Good | Good |
| Naturalness | Good | Good |
| Pronunciation | Clear and correct | Clear and correct |
| Contextual Appropriateness | Appropriate | Appropriate |

### Observations

- Both samples had clear audio with no noticeable quality issues.
- Both samples sounded natural.
- Pronunciation was clear and correct in both samples.
- Both samples were appropriate for a professional meeting context.
- Neither sample was clearly preferred overall.

### Conclusion

Both TTS samples performed similarly across the evaluated criteria. Neither sample was clearly preferred overall.

### Test Text

> Hello, your appointment is scheduled for Friday at 10 a.m. Please arrive 15 minutes early and bring your identification card.

### Evaluation Method

Both audio samples were evaluated using the same criteria: audio quality, naturalness, pronunciation, and contextual appropriateness.

[Listen to Voice D](samples/voice_d.mp3)
